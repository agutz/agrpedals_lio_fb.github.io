FUZZ TONE BENDER SHAPED FOR YOUR DAW

DOWNLOAD : LIOFUZZ BENDER VST3 PLUGIN

Two distinct fuzz voices. One fierce pedal.

LIO FUZZ BENDER brings the character of a vintage FUZZ TONE and a aggressive BENDER TONE into your DAW. Switch between a warm, dynamic germanium inspired fuzz and a more aggressive, dense silicon inspired bender, then shape the sound with pedal controls and studio friendly tone tools.

Dial in responsive attack, push the output, and use BIAS to explore different degrees of openness, compression, and harmonic character. Refine the signal with input filtering, low and high cuts, noise gate, and a clean blend for adding back your dry tone. Turn on 2X oversampling for increased processing quality, or switch it off when you prefer.

Controls and features

LIO FUZZ / LIO BENDER: Choose between the warmer, dynamic fuzz voice and the more aggressive, sustaining bender voice.

ATTACK: Adjust saturation intensity and response.

LOUD: Set the processed output level.

BIAS: Shape compression, asymmetry, and harmonic character.

FILTER: Filter the signal before distortion.

PUSH: Control the signal level feeding the fuzz circuit.

CLEAN BLEND: Mix the clean input back with the processed sound.

LOW CUT / HI CUT: Shape the processed signal’s low and high frequencies.

GATE: Reduce noise when the input signal falls below the gate setting.

2X OVERSAMPLING: Toggle 2× oversampling.

Effect ON/OFF: Toggle the pedal effect with the footswitch.

From raw, snarling fuzz to shaped, mix-ready tone—lio puts two distinct fuzz characters under your feet.**
